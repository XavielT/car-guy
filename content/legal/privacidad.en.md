# Car Guy Privacy Policy

**Version 2026-10** · effective October 1, 2026

## 1. Who is responsible

**Xaviel Terrero**, in the Dominican Republic, responsible for Car Guy (a personal project). Contact: **xavieldev@gmail.com**.

## 2. The short version

- Without an account, **your data stays on your device** (SQLite on the phone, or the browser's storage on the web). We do not receive it.
- With an account, it is copied to our server so it can sync and so you can share what you choose.
- **We do not sell your data, show ads or build advertising profiles.**

## 3. What data we process

- **Precise location**, including **in the background** (when the app is closed or not in use), **only when you turn on automatic trips**, and in the foreground when you start a trip or use drive mode. It is used to detect and record trips, compute distance and speed, and draw the route.
- **Vehicle data**: make, model, year, mileage, fuel logs, maintenance, checks, mods, track days, tires, costs, documents and notes. **Plate and VIN are optional.**
- **Trips**: date, duration, distance, speeds and the route (GPS points).
- **Photos and files**: of the vehicle, receipts, documents and your profile picture.
- **Account** (only if you create one): your e-mail, a user identifier and, if you add them, your name and avatar.
- **Feedback** you send from the app: the message, a screenshot if you attach one, the e-mail if you type it, and **device information** (model, OS version, app version, the screen you were on and technical diagnostics, with no plates, VINs or your session e-mail). We do not use the advertising ID.
- **Acceptance of these texts**: version, date, language and platform.
- App **settings** (language, units, preferences).

## 4. Why, and on what basis

- To **provide the service** you ask for: keep your history, sync it, share it with whoever you choose and answer your feedback.
- With your **consent** for location (the app asks before turning it on), photos and feedback. You can withdraw it at any time in the app or in the phone's settings.

## 5. Where it is stored

- **On your device**, always.
- **With an account**, in **Supabase** (database and private file storage), hosted on **AWS, region us-west-2, United States**. That server is shared with other apps by the same developer; Car Guy's data is kept separate and protected by per-user access rules.
- The **Car Guy website** and its server functions run on **Vercel**, which logs requests (including the IP address).

## 6. Third parties involved

- **Supabase** and **Vercel**, as providers that store and serve the data on our behalf.
- **OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap**: maps are downloaded from their servers; those requests **expose your IP address** and the map area you are viewing.
- **GitHub**: the Android APK is downloaded from GitHub Releases, which sees your IP address.
- **The people you choose**: members of a shared garage see that car, and a public page is visible to anyone with the link (showing only what you decide).

We do not share your location with anyone else.

## 7. International transfers

The servers are **outside the Dominican Republic** (United States). By creating an account you agree that your data is stored there.

## 8. How long we keep it

- **On the device**: until you delete it (in the app, with "Delete local data", or by uninstalling). **Each trip's GPS points are kept for 30 days** on the device; the trip summary stays.
- **On the server**: while you have the account. **Deleting the account** removes your data, your files and your login; copies may remain in the provider's automatic backups until those backups roll over.
- **Feedback**: while it is useful for fixing or improving the app; feedback sent with your account is deleted with it.

## 9. Your rights (Law 172-13)

Under the Dominican Republic's **Law 172-13 on the Protection of Personal Data**, you have the right to **access** your data, **rectify** it, **cancel** (erase) it and **object** to its processing.

- In the app you can see and correct everything you record, **export** it and **delete your account** (More → Account → Delete account).
- For any other request, write to **xavieldev@gmail.com**. We answer within the time limits set by the law.

## 10. Background location

- It is used only when you **turn on automatic trips**. Before asking for the permission, the app explains what it is for.
- While a trip is being recorded, Android shows a **persistent notification**.
- You can turn it off in the app (Trips → Settings) or in the phone's settings; manual trips keep working.
- It is **not shared** with third parties.

## 11. Security

Encrypted connections (TLS), per-user access rules in the database, private file storage and signed links for photos. No system is infallible; if we detect an incident that affects you, we will let you know.

## 12. Children

Car Guy is not directed at children under 13, and you must be 18 or older to create an account. If we learn that a child gave us data, we delete it.

## 13. Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, the app tells you and asks you to accept it again. The version and date are at the top.
